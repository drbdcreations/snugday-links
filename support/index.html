Snugday support

Questions, a problem with your account, or something that doesn't look right? Email team@drdbcreations.com and we'll get back to you.

Plans and billing

  • To cancel or change a subscription on iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting your account or the app doesn't cancel it.
  • If a plan doesn't show after you buy or reinstall, open Settings in Snugday, tap Plan, then Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you bought from.

Your account and data

  • To export your records, open Settings in Snugday, then Export.
  • To delete your account, open Settings in Snugday, then Delete my account.
