Snugday Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05

Snugday is an app for parents and the people who help them care for a baby or young child. You use it to keep records (feeds, diapers, sleep, growth, health notes), save photos and moments, and share them with the people you choose. This policy explains what information Snugday keeps, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Who we are. Snugday is owned by DRDBcreations and provided by KAL Foundry ("we", "us"), 16192 Coastal Highway, Lewes, DE 19958, USA. You can contact us about privacy at team@drdbcreations.com.

The short version

  • No ads in Snugday, and we never sell your information.
  • Your child's details and your family's records are never sent to analytics or advertising companies.
  • Snugday can share a device identifier and app-use events with Meta and Google to understand how the app is used and to measure and improve our marketing. In the EU, EEA and UK this is off unless you tick the box for it; elsewhere it is on unless you turn it off. Either way you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy and support.
  • Your family's records are shared only with the people you invite, and only as their role allows.
  • Health records are visible to parents only, unless a parent gives a caregiver access.
  • New memories start as Parents only. You choose who else sees each one.
  • You can export your records and delete a child's profile or your account at any time.

Information we keep

Your account

  • Your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and stored only in hashed form; we can't read them.
  • Your settings, such as appearance, units, notification choices and quiet hours.
  • Optionally, a profile photo and a color shown next to your name.

Information you add about your child

  • The child's name or nickname, date of birth and preferred units, and optionally a photo.
  • Care records: feeds, diapers, sleep, nursing timers and feed reminders, each with who logged it and when.
  • Growth records: weights and lengths, exactly as entered.
  • Health records: temperatures and illness notes. This is health information and gets extra protection (see "Health information" below).
  • Memories and milestones: photos, captions and dates, and who each one is shared with.

Your family circle

  • Who is a parent, co-parent, caregiver or family viewer for each child, and what each caregiver may see.
  • Invitations you create: the name you give the person and, if you choose to invite by email, their email address. If you invite by email, Snugday emails them the invitation, which names you and their role, but not your child. If you share a link instead, Snugday sends nothing. We remove the email address 30 days after an invitation closes.

Your plan

  • If you subscribe, the stores tell us (through RevenueCat, see below) which plan you have, whether it is in its free trial, and when it renews or ends. We never receive your card or bank details.

Where a photo was taken

  • When you add a photo to a memory or milestone, Snugday reads the date it was taken and, if the photo includes it, where. Your phone turns the location into a place name (for example "Austin, Texas") using its built-in map service (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android); Snugday stores only that name, which you can change or remove before saving, and it is shared with whoever can see that memory. The exact coordinates and all other photo metadata are removed and never stored or sent to us.

Information we do not collect

  • No device location, contacts or microphone recordings.
  • No crash-reporting or ad-serving tools are built into Snugday.

Marketing measurement with Meta

  • Snugday includes Meta's App Events SDK. Nothing is sent before you accept this policy and the terms. If your phone's region is in the EU, EEA or UK, sharing stays off until you tick the separate "Share app use with Meta and Google" box when you first open the app, or turn it on later in Settings → Privacy and support; elsewhere it starts when you accept and stays on unless you turn it off there.
  • On iPhone and iPad, nothing is shared with Meta unless you allow tracking when Apple asks; choosing "Ask App Not to Track" turns sharing with Meta off on that device, and Apple's own install reporting (SKAdNetwork) is all that remains, which identifies no one.
  • Meta receives: your device's advertising identifier, device and app information, app installs and sessions, and these events: app opened, walkthrough started or finished, account created, first child added (the fact only), setup finished (the fact only), first memory saved (the fact only), invitation sent (the fact only), plan screen viewed, checkout started with product and price, and a trial, a purchase or a trial becoming a paid plan, each with product, price and order number.
  • Meta never receives your name, email, or anything about your child or family: no names, birth dates, photos, memories, milestones, care, growth or health records.
  • We use this to learn which marketing brings people to Snugday and which of them go on to use it. Meta handles this information under its own terms and privacy policy and may use it to measure and improve advertising, including showing ads for Snugday elsewhere.
  • This does reveal to Meta that you use a baby-care app. Snugday works exactly the same if you turn it off.

App-use analytics with Google

  • Snugday uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used, and Firebase Remote Config to try out different versions of a screen. Both sit behind the same "Share app use" setting as Meta: nothing is sent or fetched before you accept this policy and the terms, it stays off in the EU, EEA and UK unless you tick the box or turn it on, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy and support.
  • Google receives: an identifier for this copy of the app, device and app information, and app-use events: the steps of first-time setup, signing in, plan screens and purchases, invitations, and which tab was opened. It also records which version of a screen you were shown during a test.
  • Google never receives your name, email, advertising identifier or anything about your child or family: no names, birth dates, photos, memories, milestones, care, growth or health records, and not which feature you tried to use.
  • We use no advertising features of Google Analytics and don't combine this with data from other companies, so on iPhone and iPad it isn't tracking in Apple's sense. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" stops sharing with Meta but not this; the setting in Settings stops both.

On your phone

  • Snugday keeps a copy of your family's records on your phone so it works offline, and syncs it when you're back online. Signing out removes that copy from the phone.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone's own notification system.

How we use information

Apart from the marketing measurement and app-use analytics described above, we use your information only to provide Snugday:

  • to store and sync your records and photos, and show them to the people you've shared them with;
  • to enforce who can see and change what, on our servers, not only in the app;
  • to run your plan and the free trial;
  • to send the account emails you need (confirming your email, resetting your password) and the invitations you ask us to email;
  • to keep Snugday secure and to answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your family's records for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who can see your family's information

  • People you invite see a child's information according to their role:
    • Parents and co-parents: everything for that child.
    • Caregivers: care records, plus growth, health or adding memories only if a parent allows it.
    • Family viewers: only the memories and milestones shared with them.
  • Access is set per child. Removing someone takes effect right away.
  • We (a small number of people at Snugday) can access stored information only when needed to run the service, to fix a problem you report, or when the law requires it.

Health information

Temperatures and illness notes are health information. Snugday stores them for a child only after a parent has given explicit consent in the app: before the first temperature or illness note for each child, a parent is shown a separate box, not part of accepting these terms, and must tick it. We keep a record of who consented, for which child, when, and which version of the wording; if the wording changes, we ask again. You can withdraw that consent at any time by deleting the child's health records in the app (each record, or the child's profile); withdrawing doesn't affect what was done before.

These records are visible only to a child's parents and co-parents, plus any caregiver a parent has specifically given access to. When a parent gives a caregiver that access, the app says what it allows; before the caregiver can see or add a child's health records, they are asked, once for each child, to acknowledge that this is private health information to be used only to care for that child. We keep a record of who acknowledged, for which child, when, and which version of the wording; if access is removed and later given back, or the wording changes, they are asked again. Snugday keeps these records as you enter them and doesn't interpret them or give medical advice.

Children

Snugday is meant to be used by adults. Children don't create accounts. Information about a child is added and controlled by the child's parents, who can view, export, correct or delete it at any time. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

  • Your information is kept while your account is open.
  • Deleting a child's profile or your account hides it at once. You can undo it for 30 days; after that it is permanently deleted, including photos.
  • Copies in our provider's backups expire within 7 days, so deleted information is gone from backups no later than 37 days after you delete it.
  • If you remove someone from a child's circle, they lose access right away and the copy on their phone is removed when it next connects.

Your choices and rights

In the app you can, at any time:

  • see and correct your information and your child's records;
  • export records as PDF or CSV, with photos (Settings → Export);
  • change who sees each memory, and remove people from a child's circle;
  • delete a child's profile or your account (Settings).

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or other places with privacy laws), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of your information, to receive it in a portable form, and to complain to your data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us at team@drdbcreations.com. We will answer within the time the law requires. We don't sell personal information. You can turn sharing with Meta and Google on or off any time in Settings → Privacy and support; on iPhone and iPad, refusing tracking in your phone's settings also stops sharing with Meta. Turning it off stops further sharing; to ask Meta about what it already holds, use Meta's own privacy tools.

Legal bases (EU, EEA and UK). We rely on our contract with you to provide Snugday; on your explicit consent for a child's health information (given by a parent in the app) and for marketing measurement with Meta and app-use analytics with Google (the separate box, which you can untick at any time); and on our legitimate interest in keeping Snugday secure and running.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Who can see and change what is enforced on our servers for every request. Photos are stored privately and are only served to people allowed to see them. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
